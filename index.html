Privacy Policy for Gold Tracker

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Gold Tracker Android app ("the app", "we") handles information. We designed the app to work without collecting personal data. This policy applies to the app and its home screen widgets.

1. What the app does

Gold Tracker shows the gold and silver board rates published by the Madras Jewellers & Diamond Traders Association (MJDTA), a Tamil Nadu regional benchmark. It shows today's rate, how it compares with earlier days, a rate history and home screen widgets. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by MJDTA. The rates are for general information only and are not financial advice.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell, share or transmit any personal or sensitive user data. We do not run any servers for this app. Specifically, the app does not:

  • require you to create an account or sign in;
  • collect your name, email address, phone number or postal address;
  • access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, calendar or messages;
  • use advertising, advertising IDs or ad networks;
  • use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services;
  • process payments.

3. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your own device. It never leaves your device through the app:

  • the gold and silver rate history downloaded from MJDTA, so the app can work offline;
  • your app settings, such as the sync interval and the Wi-Fi-only option;
  • the status of the last sync.

Android's backup feature is turned off for this app. When you uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, all of this data is deleted.

4. Network requests and third parties

To fetch the latest rates, the app sends standard HTTPS requests directly from your device to the public MJDTA rates website. Like any website, MJDTA can see technical information that is part of every internet request, such as your device's IP address and the app's user-agent text. We do not receive this information and we do not control how MJDTA handles it. Please refer to the MJDTA website for its own policies. The app sends no personal data, device identifiers or usage information in these requests.

We do not share information with any other third party. The app does not include third-party SDKs that collect data.

6. Data security

All network requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. Cleartext (unencrypted) traffic is disabled in the app. Because data stays on your device and we collect no personal data, there is no personal data held by us to be exposed.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep no data about you. The rate history and settings on your device remain until you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings (Settings > Apps > Gold Tracker > Storage > Clear storage). No request to us is needed, because we hold nothing to delete.

8. Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose information tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or export. If you are in a region with privacy laws that give you such rights, you may still contact us using the email above and we will respond.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding a feature that collects data, we will update this policy and the effective date at the top before releasing that version. Please review it from time to time.
